About Us

Journalism, Without the Inconvenience of Reality.

The Banana Times is an independent satirical publication dedicated to reporting on a world that frequently makes satire unnecessary.

We cover politics, technology, business, culture, and the everyday absurdities of modern life with the gravity normally reserved for matters of national importance.

Our stories are fictional. Our observations are not always.

Our Editorial Philosophy

We believe every story deserves careful consideration, particularly those that should never have been stories in the first place.

Our editorial approach combines the discipline of traditional journalism with a willingness to examine the ridiculous.

We maintain high standards of fictional reporting and reserve the right to be deeply concerned about things that do not exist.

A Note to Readers

The Banana Times publishes satire. Its articles are fictional works created for humor, commentary, and entertainment.

References to real events, institutions, or public figures should be understood within this satirical context.

Nothing published here should be mistaken for factual news reporting.
