For Vijay Salgaonkar, Giving Up Gutkha Proved Harder Than Evading the Police

The central figure in the Drishyam films has repeatedly outwitted investigators. A close friend says overcoming a longstanding habit has been a different matter.

By The Banana Times Staff · October 20, 2026

PANAJI, Goa — Vijay Salgaonkar, the cable television operator whose ability to evade police investigations has become the subject of considerable attention, has faced a more persistent challenge in his personal life, according to a close friend: giving up gutkha.

The friend, who said he had known Salgaonkar for years, described the habit as a source of growing frustration for his family.

“He managed to mislead senior police officers, construct an elaborate alibi and persuade his entire family to remain silent,” the friend said. “But he could not stay away from gutkha for two hours.”

Salgaonkar had considered quitting on several occasions, the friend said, but each attempt was interrupted by developments in the police investigation.

“Whenever he made up his mind to quit, the police reopened the case,” he said. “Under those circumstances, he felt it was not the right time.”

His family, however, has reportedly grown less sympathetic.

According to the friend, Salgaonkar's wife had become particularly concerned about the packets concealed throughout their home.

“She told him that hiding a body was one thing,” the friend recalled. “But hiding gutkha packets around the house was becoming unacceptable.”

Despite these difficulties, the friend expressed confidence that Salgaonkar would eventually overcome the habit.

“He has managed to escape the police three times,” he said. “His family is beginning to think that giving up gutkha would be a greater achievement.”
