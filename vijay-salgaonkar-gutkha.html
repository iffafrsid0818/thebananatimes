Close Friend of Vijay Salgaonkar Reveals Why Gutkha Addiction Was a Bigger Problem Than Escaping Police

Friend claims the Drishyam protagonist could fool an entire police department but struggled to give up one habit.

By The Banana Times Staff · October 20, 2026

PANAJI, GOA — A close friend of Vijay Salgaonkar has claimed that the cable operator's greatest struggle was not escaping the police in Drishyam, but overcoming his addiction to gutkha.

“He managed to fool senior police officers, fabricate an alibi and keep his entire family silent for years,” the friend said. “But he couldn't stay away from gutkha for two hours.”

According to the friend, Salgaonkar considered quitting several times but repeatedly postponed the decision because of ongoing investigations.

“Every time he decided to quit, the police reopened the case,” he said. “He needed something to manage the stress.”

The friend said Salgaonkar's family had grown increasingly frustrated.

“His wife told him that hiding a body was one thing, but hiding gutkha packets around the house was unacceptable.”

Asked whether Salgaonkar had finally overcome the addiction, the friend said he remained optimistic.

“He has escaped the police three times,” the friend said. “At this point, his family believes quitting gutkha would be the biggest achievement of his life.”
