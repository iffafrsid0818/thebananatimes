Man Sues AI Company Over Dispute About His Appearance in 1980

Raghav Mehta says an AI reconstruction of an old photograph made him less handsome than he remembers, although the photographs have introduced an incongruous complication.

By The Banana Times Staff · September 30, 2026

MUMBAI — A 67-year-old man has sued an artificial-intelligence company after asking its software to recreate how he looked in 1980 and receiving a portrait that he says made him considerably less handsome.

The difference, Raghav Mehta said, was so incongruous with his memory of himself that the experience left him psychologically distressed.

“I know what I looked like,” Mehta said. “But everyone keeps telling me to use AI because it is never wrong. So now I don’t know whom to believe — myself or the computer.”

Mehta’s wife disagreed. After examining both photographs, she said the AI-generated version of her husband was more handsome.

Mehta dismissed her assessment and asked her to go to the kitchen and make him a snack, saying he was hungry.

At one point, Mehta asked the chatbot whether banging the computer might correct the error. It advised against doing so, noting that physical damage was “unlikely to improve historical accuracy.”

His lawyers submitted Mehta’s original photograph and the AI-generated portrait to the court as evidence.

Axiom Intelligence offered Mehta an undisclosed settlement and proposed naming its next model, Raghav-4, after him. He refused both, saying accuracy was more important than money or recognition.

Axiom has since announced a $3 billion investment to improve what it calls “historical facial accuracy.”

Mehta welcomed the investment.

“I just want the truth,” he said.
