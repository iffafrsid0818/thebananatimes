Forensic Team Called After Suspected Midnight Birthday Celebration on Tank Bund

Investigators examine fingerprints, DNA and CCTV footage after signs of a late-night birthday celebration are found along Hussain Sagar Lake.

By The Banana Times Staff · October 12, 2026

HYDERABAD — A forensic team was called to Tank Bund, along Hussain Sagar Lake, early Monday after police found what they described as evidence of a birthday celebration that may have taken place shortly after midnight.

Officers recovered a cake box, three paper plates, a plastic knife and the remains of several candles. The items were examined for fingerprints, and investigators collected a DNA sample from a partially eaten piece of chocolate cake.

Police declined to say whether the sample had produced a match.

“The investigation is still underway,” one officer said. “It would be premature to comment further.”

A constable assigned to the area at the time said he had not seen anything unusual.

“I swear to God, I did not see anyone,” he said.

He also said he had not heard singing, clapping or anyone calling for “one more photo.”

Investigators later questioned a man who appeared in nearby CCTV footage. He was released after police learned that he was a strict vegetarian and that the cake recovered from the scene contained egg.

“This changes the direction of the investigation,” an officer said. “At this stage, we have no evidence that he would voluntarily eat this cake.”

The case has also produced an unexpected response from local bakeries.

One cake vendor said demand for regular cakes had fallen sharply in recent days, while requests for eggless cakes had increased.

“Earlier, people asked for eggless because of religion or diet,” he said. “Now they are asking because of evidence.”

Police are continuing to review CCTV footage and are trying to determine who purchased the cake, who brought it to Tank Bund and who made the first cut.

No arrests have been made.

The cake remains in police custody.
