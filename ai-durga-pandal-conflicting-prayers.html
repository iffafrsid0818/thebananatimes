AI Durga Pandal Faces First Crisis After Family Makes Conflicting Prayers

A virtual Durga Puja pandal promises less traffic, less waste and AI-assisted prayers, but its testing phase has exposed one problem the developers did not anticipate: Indian families.

By The Banana Times Staff · October 6, 2026

KOLKATA — A Gen Z Durga Puja committee testing its new AI-powered virtual pandal encountered its first major technical problem after members of the same family made completely incompatible prayers.

The virtual pandal allows devotees to enter through VR, avoid traffic and queues, and offer their prayers through an AI system.

During testing, a mother asked Maa Durga to help her son secure a permanent government job in Kolkata.

Minutes later, the son’s wife entered from another headset and prayed that her husband receive a transfer “as far from Kolkata as administratively possible.”

“Conflicting blessings detected. Indian values prevent us from choosing sides.”

The AI reportedly spent several minutes processing both requests before displaying the message.

Developers said the incident revealed a major limitation of artificial intelligence.

“We trained it on religion, philosophy and government recruitment rules,” one developer said. “We forgot about Indian families.”

Traditional pandal makers, already unhappy about the virtual project, said this proved technology could never replace a real pandal.

“At a real pandal,” one craftsman said, “both prayers would remain pending without any notification.”

A religious influencer said the incident may also explain why so many prayers go unanswered in real life.

“We are constantly praying for things that cancel each other out,” he said. “One person wants rain, another wants sunshine. One wants promotion, another wants the same promotion. We are still a developing nation because even our prayers have not reached a common consensus. We must progress together as a society.”
